# Task 1 — Identified Operations

The following operations are derived from the Smart Museum Artifact Conservation System scenario. State names are not used as operation names.

| # | Operation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PerformSelfCheck | Checks essential sensors and environmental-control devices when the chamber is powered on. |
| 2 | CheckSensorStatus | Verifies that required sensors are functioning correctly. |
| 3 | RecordArtifactInformation | Records the artifact identification information when an artifact is placed inside. |
| 4 | LoadEnvironmentalProfile | Loads the required environmental limits for the artifact. |
| 5 | MonitorEnvironment | Continuously monitors the chamber and artifact environmental conditions. |
| 6 | CheckDoorStatus | Determines whether the chamber door is open or closed. |
| 7 | MeasureTemperature | Reads and evaluates the current chamber temperature against the artifact limits. |
| 8 | MeasureHumidity | Reads and evaluates the current chamber humidity against the artifact limits. |
| 9 | CorrectTemperature | Activates environmental controls to restore temperature to the permitted range. |
| 10 | CorrectHumidity | Activates environmental controls to restore humidity to the permitted range. |
| 11 | VerifyEnvironmentalRecovery | Confirms through sensor readings that a corrected environmental condition has actually returned to its permitted range. |
| 12 | DetectVibration | Detects significant vibration while an artifact is inside the chamber. |
| 13 | VerifyVibrationStabilization | Confirms that vibration remains below the permitted threshold for the required stabilization period. |
| 14 | SuspendConservationActivities | Immediately suspends normal conservation activities when required, such as when the door is opened or significant vibration occurs. |
| 15 | ActivateProtectionControls | Reduces light exposure and activates additional environmental controls when protection is required. |
| 16 | GenerateOperatorAlert | Generates an alert for the museum operator when a protection response is required. |
| 17 | SwitchToEmergencyPower | Switches the chamber to an emergency power source when normal power is lost and emergency power is available. |
| 18 | RecordPowerFailure | Records a power-loss incident when normal power is lost. |
| 19 | VerifySafeCondition | Verifies environmental conditions, sensor status, door status, and protection status before safe removal or normal operation. |
| 20 | AuthorizeArtifactRemoval | Allows artifact removal only after the system confirms that the chamber is safe and no active protection response is underway. |
| 21 | PerformSafeShutdown | Safely shuts down the chamber when power is lost and emergency power is unavailable. |
